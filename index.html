Start your logbook

  1. In the NHS App, download letters from Documents, and screenshot your GP health record pages (allergies, medicines, conditions, test results).
  2. Tap Import documents or photos. Add the PDFs and screenshots, or photograph paper letters. Claude reads them, summarises each letter and lists what it found for you to check.
  3. Add anything else by hand: symptoms as they happen, upcoming appointments, what the doctor said.

For your complete record, you can ask your GP surgery for a copy (a subject access request). It's free.

                Get ready for an appointment

                Claude reads your logbook and writes a short brief: what to say first, a timeline, the medicines to mention, and questions to ask.

                Look at the whole picture

                For when appointments keep going round in circles. Claude reviews everything in your logbook and suggests what might tie it together, what hasn't been checked yet, and how to ask for it.

                Talk it through

                Ask what could be behind a symptom, what a result or letter means, or how to push for a test. Claude can see your logbook. Chats aren't saved.
