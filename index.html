My health

Start your logbook

  1. In the NHS App, download letters from Documents, and screenshot your GP health record pages (allergies, medicines, conditions, test results).
  2. Tap Import documents or photos. Add the PDFs and screenshots, or photograph paper letters. Dr Quackers reads them, summarises each letter and lists what it found for you to check.
  3. Add anything else by hand: symptoms as they happen, upcoming appointments, what the doctor said.

For your complete record, you can ask your GP surgery for a copy (a subject access request). It's free.

This logbook helps you keep track and prepare for appointments. It isn't medical advice. In an emergency call 999. For urgent help that isn't an emergency, call NHS 111.
